Texture
Every image sits in a hard ink frame with a 5px halftone screen over it, multiply blend. Unearned slots are a stone-grey bust silhouette on paper — shape only, no features. Nothing gets a soft shadow; depth is a 3px offset block of ink.
Icons & marks

No drawn icons. Marks are single characters set in a square frame — brush face for seals and tiers, sans for functional chips. Departure uses a dashed frame and never a red mark.
Rules of the reward layer
Nothing on any screen states or implies a difficulty ladder. HSK level never appears next to a hero, a slot, or a count.
Setbacks are inventory, not loss. Departed heroes keep their name and their slot, and the copy points at return rather than failure.
Chinese is content: never below 32px on a card, never used as decoration behind text, always with pinyin and English beneath it.
Turn 3 · Water Margin direction
Woodblock heroes
The Suikoden print treated as an unrolled scroll: aged paper ground with visible fibre, hairline ink rules, wooden rods top and bottom, seal-red marks, brush script on the daily-word banner, and heavy Archivo display type. Buttons are gilt on seal red — the 聚义 pairing — since dark type on coral never had the contrast. Prints keep the hard ink frame and full halftone, unobstructed. The page now leads with the quest — climb the mountain, unite the 108 — then subscribing, sample words, and how the game works. Brush script carries the 好 mark and the section marks; buttons are pale blue drawn from the prints, red held back for seals and completions. Same structure as 1a; immersion shares the toggle from 2a.
